# Workflows

## Overview

Terraform Workflows define the processes used to validate, review, deploy, and manage infrastructure changes.

Well-defined workflows improve consistency, reduce operational risk, and help teams safely manage infrastructure throughout its lifecycle.

Workflows are a critical part of production Terraform usage.

---

## Why Workflows Matter

Without Workflows:

```text
Infrastructure Changes
          ↓
Manual Operations
          ↓
Operational Risk
```

With Workflows:

```text
Infrastructure Changes
          ↓
Validation
          ↓
Review
          ↓
Deployment
          ↓
Managed Operations
```

Benefits:

- Consistency
- Repeatability
- Governance
- Reduced Risk
- Team Collaboration

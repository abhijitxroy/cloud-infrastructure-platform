# Security Overview

## Overview

Security is the practice of protecting systems, applications, infrastructure, data, and operational environments from unauthorized access, misuse, disruption, and cyber threats.

Modern cloud platforms, Kubernetes environments, enterprise systems, and distributed applications rely on security controls to protect business operations and sensitive information.

Security is a foundational discipline for platform engineering, cloud engineering, DevOps, and site reliability engineering.

---

## Why Security Matters

Without Security:

```text
Systems
   ↓
Threats And Vulnerabilities
   ↓
Unauthorized Access
   ↓
Operational Risk
```

With Security:

```text
Systems
   ↓
Security Controls
   ↓
Protected Resources
   ↓
Reduced Risk
```

Benefits:

- Infrastructure Protection
- Data Protection
- Reduced Security Risk
- Regulatory Compliance
- Operational Reliability

---

## Core Security Principles

### Confidentiality

Ensures information is accessible only to authorized users.

Examples:

- Encryption
- Access Controls
- Secrets Management

---

### Integrity

Ensures information remains accurate and unmodified.

Examples:

- Checksums
- Digital Signatures
- Audit Logs

---

### Availability

Ensures systems and services remain accessible when needed.

Examples:

- Backups
- Redundancy
- Disaster Recovery

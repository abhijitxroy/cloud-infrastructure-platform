# Compliance

## Overview

Compliance is the process of ensuring systems, applications, infrastructure, and operational practices meet regulatory, legal, organizational, and security requirements.

Compliance helps organizations reduce risk, protect sensitive data, maintain customer trust, and demonstrate adherence to established standards and regulations.

Compliance is an important part of cloud security, platform engineering, governance, and enterprise operations.

---

## Why Compliance Matters

Without Compliance:

```text
Systems
   ↓
Uncontrolled Processes
   ↓
Regulatory Risk
```

With Compliance:

```text
Systems
   ↓
Security Controls
   ↓
Compliance Requirements
   ↓
Risk Reduction
```

Benefits:

- Risk Reduction
- Better Governance
- Customer Trust
- Regulatory Alignment
- Improved Security Practices

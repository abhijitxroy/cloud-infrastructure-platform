

# Compliance Basics

## Overview

Compliance is the process of ensuring systems, applications, infrastructure, and operational practices meet regulatory, legal, organizational, and security requirements.

Compliance helps organizations protect data, reduce risk, maintain customer trust, and demonstrate adherence to established standards and regulations.

Compliance is an important aspect of cloud security, platform engineering, enterprise operations, and governance.

---

## Why Compliance Matters

Without Compliance:

```text
Systems
   ↓
Uncontrolled Processes
   ↓
Regulatory Risk
   ↓
Business Impact
```

With Compliance:

```text
Systems
   ↓
Security Controls
   ↓
Compliance Requirements
   ↓
Risk Reduction
```

Benefits:

- Risk Reduction
- Regulatory Alignment
- Better Governance
- Customer Trust
- Improved Security Practices

## Common Compliance Areas

### Data Protection

Protects sensitive information.

Examples:

- Personal Data
- Customer Information
- Financial Records
- Business Data

---

### Access Management

Ensures only authorized users can access resources.

Examples:

- IAM Controls
- Least Privilege Access
- Access Reviews

---

### Auditability

Provides evidence of operational and security activities.

Examples:

- Audit Logs
- Activity Tracking
- Change Records

---

### Security Controls

Protect systems and data.

Examples:

- Encryption
- Network Security
- Vulnerability Management

---

## Common Compliance Frameworks

Examples:

- SOC 2
- ISO 27001
- PCI DSS
- GDPR
- HIPAA

Understanding what these frameworks aim to protect is more important than memorizing every requirement.

## Compliance vs Security

| Area | Compliance | Security |
|--------|------------|----------|
| Goal | Meet Requirements | Protect Systems |
| Focus | Standards And Regulations | Risk Reduction |
| Measurement | Audits And Evidence | Security Posture |
| Scope | Governance | Protection |



# Reliability

## Overview

Reliability focuses on ensuring systems consistently deliver expected functionality under normal and failure conditions.

Reliable systems minimize downtime, maintain service availability, and provide a predictable user experience.

Reliability is a core objective of observability, site reliability engineering (SRE), and platform operations.

---

## Why Reliability Matters

Without Reliability:

```text
Service Issues
      ↓
Frequent Outages
      ↓
Poor User Experience
```

With Reliability:

```text
Service Issues
      ↓
Detection
      ↓
Response
      ↓
Stable Services
```

Benefits:

- Improved Availability
- Better User Experience
- Reduced Downtime
- Predictable Operations
- Stronger Business Continuity

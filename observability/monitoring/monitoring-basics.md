# Monitoring Basics

## Overview

Monitoring helps engineering teams track infrastructure health, workload behavior and operational conditions across systems.

Modern infrastructure environments commonly depend on monitoring capabilities to improve reliability, availability and operational awareness.

Monitoring becomes increasingly important as infrastructure systems evolve toward distributed platforms and cloud native environments.

---

## Common Monitoring Areas

Examples:

- Infrastructure health
- Application availability
- Resource utilization
- Service responsiveness
- Operational status

---

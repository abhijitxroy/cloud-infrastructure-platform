# Monitoring Basics

## Overview

Monitoring is the process of continuously collecting, analyzing, and visualizing information about systems, applications, infrastructure, and services.

Monitoring helps engineering teams detect issues, understand system health, improve reliability, and maintain operational visibility.

Monitoring is one of the foundational capabilities of modern observability platforms.

---

## Why Monitoring Matters

Without Monitoring:

```text
System Issue
      ↓
No Visibility
      ↓
Delayed Detection
```

With Monitoring:

```text
System Issue
      ↓
Monitoring
      ↓
Early Detection
      ↓
Faster Resolution
```

Benefits:

- Faster Issue Detection
- Improved Reliability
- Better Operational Visibility
- Reduced Downtime
- Proactive Operations

## Monitoring vs Observability

| Feature | Monitoring | Observability |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| Goal | Detect Issues | Understand Issues |
| Focus | Known Problems | Known And Unknown Problems |
| Data Sources | Primarily Metrics | Metrics, Logs, Traces |
| Investigation Depth | Limited | Deep Analysis |

# Observability Overview

## Overview

Observability helps engineering teams understand application behavior, infrastructure health and operational conditions across distributed systems.

Modern infrastructure environments commonly rely on observability capabilities to improve reliability, troubleshooting efficiency and operational awareness.

Observability becomes increasingly important as engineering systems evolve toward cloud platforms, container environments and distributed architectures.

---

## Common Observability Areas

Examples:

- Logging
- Monitoring
- Metrics
- Tracing
- Operational visibility

---

## Operational Objectives

Observability commonly supports:

- Infrastructure visibility
- System reliability
- Issue investigation
- Performance understanding
- Operational awareness

---

## Infrastructure Integration Areas

Examples:

- Cloud platforms
- Container environments
- CI/CD systems
- Platform engineering environments

---

## Notes

Observability practices become increasingly important as infrastructure systems grow in scale, complexity and operational dependency.

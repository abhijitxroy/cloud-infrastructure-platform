# Registry Operations

## Overview

Registry operations focus on maintaining container registry environments and supporting reliable artifact lifecycle workflows.

Operational management helps engineering teams maintain registry availability, artifact consistency and delivery reliability across infrastructure environments.

Registry operations become increasingly important as engineering systems expand across cloud platforms and container ecosystems.

---

## Common Operational Areas

Examples:

- Artifact lifecycle management
- Registry availability
- Access management
- Operational maintenance
- Storage lifecycle management

# Registry Operations

## Overview

Registry operations focus on managing, maintaining, monitoring, and optimizing container registry platforms used for software delivery and container image distribution.

Modern engineering organizations rely on registry operations to ensure image availability, platform reliability, security compliance, and efficient lifecycle management.

Registry operations are a critical capability for Kubernetes platforms, Docker environments, CI/CD systems, and cloud-native infrastructure.

---

## Why Registry Operations Matter

Without Registry Operations:

```text
Container Registry
        ↓
Operational Issues
        ↓
Image Availability Risk
        ↓
Deployment Failures
```

With Registry Operations:

```text
Container Registry
        ↓
Monitoring And Management
        ↓
Reliable Operations
        ↓
Successful Deployments
```

Benefits:

- Registry Reliability
- Image Availability
- Operational Visibility
- Capacity Management
- Platform Stability

---

## Core Operational Areas

### Registry Availability

Registry platforms must remain available so deployment systems can retrieve required container images.

---

### Storage Management

Registry storage must be monitored and managed to support growth and control operational costs.

---

### Capacity Planning

Engineering teams must forecast storage growth, image volume, and registry usage patterns.

---

### Monitoring

Operational visibility helps detect performance issues, failures, and service degradation.

---

### Image Cleanup

Unused and outdated images should be managed through lifecycle and retention policies.

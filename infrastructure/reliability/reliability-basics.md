# Reliability Basics

## Overview

Reliability focuses on maintaining stable infrastructure behavior and predictable system operation across engineering environments.

Reliable infrastructure helps applications remain available and operational under expected workload conditions and changing operational demands.

Reliability becomes increasingly important as infrastructure systems evolve toward cloud platforms, distributed architectures and large scale operational environments.

---

## Common Reliability Areas

Examples:

- Infrastructure stability
- Service availability
- Failure reduction
- Operational consistency
- Platform continuity

---

## Reliability Objectives

Reliability practices commonly support:

- Reduced operational disruption
- Stable application delivery
- Predictable infrastructure behavior
- Platform consistency

# Infrastructure Overview

## Overview

Infrastructure provides the foundational systems required to support application execution, operational workflows and engineering platforms.

Modern infrastructure environments commonly combine compute resources, networking, storage and operational systems to deliver reliable engineering platforms.

Infrastructure architecture becomes increasingly important as systems evolve toward cloud environments and distributed operational models.

---

## Common Infrastructure Areas

Examples:

- Compute platforms
- Networking systems
- Storage environments
- Security systems
- Platform services

---

## Operational Objectives

Infrastructure commonly supports:

- Reliability
- Availability
- Operational consistency
- Scalability
- Platform stability

---

## Infrastructure Integration Areas

Examples:

- Cloud platforms
- Container environments
- Platform engineering systems
- Infrastructure operations

---

## Notes

Infrastructure design becomes increasingly important as engineering systems expand across cloud platforms and operational ownership models.

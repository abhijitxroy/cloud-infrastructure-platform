# Capacity Planning

## Overview

Capacity planning is the process of forecasting infrastructure requirements to ensure systems can support current workloads and future growth.

Engineering teams use capacity planning to balance performance, reliability, scalability, and cost while maintaining operational stability.

Capacity planning is a foundational discipline across cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, distributed systems, Kubernetes platforms, and large-scale production environments.

---

## Why Capacity Planning Matters

Without Capacity Planning:

```text
Workload Growth
        ↓
Resource Exhaustion
        ↓
Performance Degradation
        ↓
Service Disruption
```

With Capacity Planning:

```text
Workload Growth
        ↓
Demand Forecasting
        ↓
Capacity Adjustments
        ↓
Reliable Operations
```

Benefits:

- Better Reliability
- Improved Performance
- Predictable Scaling
- Cost Optimization
- Operational Stability

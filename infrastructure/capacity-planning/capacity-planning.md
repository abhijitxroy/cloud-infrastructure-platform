# Capacity Planning

## Overview

Capacity planning focuses on evaluating infrastructure requirements to support current operational demand and future workload growth.

Engineering environments commonly apply capacity planning practices to improve reliability, operational efficiency and infrastructure stability.

Capacity planning becomes increasingly important as infrastructure systems expand across cloud platforms and distributed operational environments.

---

## Common Objectives

Capacity planning commonly supports:

- Infrastructure stability
- Performance consistency
- Resource optimization
- Operational continuity
- Growth readiness

---

## Capacity Areas

Examples:

### Compute Capacity

Infrastructure processing capability requirements.

Examples:

- CPU resources
- Memory allocation
- Compute scaling requirements

---

### Storage Capacity

Storage sizing and retention planning.

Examples:

- Persistent storage growth
- Backup lifecycle requirements
- Data retention planning

---

### Network Capacity

Infrastructure communication and traffic planning.

Examples:

- Traffic growth patterns
- Network utilization
- Connectivity requirements

---

## Capacity Planning Workflow

Example flow:

Operational Demand Analysis

↓

Resource Utilization Review

↓

Growth Forecast

↓

Capacity Planning Decision

↓

Infrastructure Adjustment

Capacity planning helps engineering teams prepare infrastructure environments for operational growth.
